History

We started in 2001 with a dream of exploring the wildest rivers. With a small team of passionate adventurers, we’ve grown into a leading rafting company known for safety, excitement, and breathtaking nature experiences. Over the years, we’ve guided thousands of thrill-seekers and continue to expand our river adventures while maintaining the highest safety standards.
